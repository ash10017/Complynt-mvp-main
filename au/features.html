Core Features

Your entire compliance stack,
in one place.

Automated Renewal Tracking

Every food safety registration, liquor licence, Fair Work obligation, BAS lodgement, and Super deadline tracked automatically. Alerts at 90, 60, 30, and 7 days before expiry — via email. Never pay a late-renewal penalty again.

Real-time Risk Score

See which licences are at highest risk before an inspector or auditor arrives. Know before you're caught.

Financial Exposure

See exactly what a lapse costs. VCGLR violations start at A$10,000. Super Guarantee Charge adds 10% interest. Know your risk in dollars.

Document Vault

Upload and store every compliance document — licences, food safety certificates, council approvals, insurance, inspection reports — in one secure place. When council or WorkSafe walks in, you're ready in 30 seconds, not 30 minutes of frantic searching.

Multi-Venue & Team Management Scale plan

Managing more than one venue? Complynt handles compliance across every location from a single view. Assign tasks to your team, track document uploads, and stay on top of every site's status — without a single spreadsheet.
